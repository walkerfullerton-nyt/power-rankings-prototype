Messi scores Inter Miami's first goal at Nu Stadium, Suárez salvages draw vs. Austin FC

Lionel Messi may not have scored the first goal at Inter Miami's new Nu Stadium, but he scored the home club's first goal. Four minutes after Guilherme Biro spoiled Inter Miami's party with a sixth-minute header from a corner kick, Messi answered.

His 67th-minute strike — a left-footed curler from the edge of the area — drew the largest roar of the night from the 72,000 in attendance. The goal was his ninth of the 2025 MLS season, and with it came this:

Luis Suárez then leveled in the 81st minute, heading home a Messi cross to make it 2–2 — a result that felt like a missed opportunity for a Miami side that dominated long stretches of the second half.

The new 72,000-seat stadium, which replaced Chase Stadium, had been built partly to accommodate the demand that Messi's arrival generated. On nights like this, with the crowd on its feet for every Messi touch, the investment already seems justified.
